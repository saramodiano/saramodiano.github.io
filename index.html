About

I'm Sara Modiano — a medical student living in NYC. I enjoy slowing down and seeing the world through the lens of my camera–mostly the iPhone in my pocket, but sometimes, if I think ahead, my Canon.

Outside of photography, I enjoy cooking and baking in my mini-kitchenette, and designing my small space. Most of the time, however, you can find me working in the hospital or studying. I hope to be the kind of doctor that sees past a disease or diagnosis, to capture a candid snapshot of the whole person in front of me.

Before any of this, I studied Biological Engineering at MIT where I spent hours in the student pottery studio. And before that, I filmed and edited lots of fun videos, including co-founding my high school's video channel.
